# Recorded sound

`release_2.wav` (the cartridge eject) is a trimmed, leveled recording from "51 UI sound
effects (buttons, switches and clicks)" by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), CC0 —
https://opengameart.org/content/51-ui-sound-effects-buttons-switches-and-clicks

All other sounds are synthesized by tools/make_sounds.py, modeled on this recording.
